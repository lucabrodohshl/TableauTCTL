\section{What does not work, and why}
\label{sec:negative}

This section exists so nobody spends a second week on these. Each was
implemented and measured, not reasoned about.

\subsection{Zone subsumption}

Realisability is monotone under narrowing, so an accepted verdict transfers to
every sub-zone and a rejected one to every super-zone --- the passed-list
inclusion test of a reachability tool. Implemented, it gave $2\%$ fewer nodes
and cost slightly more than it saved.

The reason is structural: the several zones a discrete shape carries are the
\emph{disjoint} cells that the delay rule produces, and disjoint zones are never
subsets of one another. Inclusion has nothing to fire on.

\subsection{Coarser abstraction ($\mathrm{Extra}_{LU}$)}

Strictly coarser than $\mathrm{Extra}_M$ and already available in the DBM
library. It cannot help as the construction stands: the split points a clock is
tested against are used as \emph{equalities} by the delay rule, so a clock's
lower and upper bound sets coincide and $\mathrm{Extra}_{LU}$ collapses to
$\mathrm{Extra}_M$. It would need the splitting reworked to be one-sided first.

\subsection{Forced stops}

If $\psi$ is already a goal and the whole zone lies inside the window, the stop
branch is available at every valuation and satisfies the until outright, so the
deferral branch looks redundant. It is not. Two satisfiable formulas turn
unsatisfiable:
\[
  \mathrm{AG}\,\mathrm{AF}_{[0,3]}q \wedge
  \mathrm{AG}(q\rightarrow \mathrm{AG}_{(0,1]}\neg q),
  \qquad
  \mathrm{AG}(p\wedge \mathrm{A}(q\,\mathbf{U}_{[1,3]}\,r)) \wedge
  \mathrm{EG}(r\rightarrow\mathrm{EG}\,r).
\]
Incomplete rather than unsound, so it never lies about \textsc{unsat} --- and
the node counts before the check were tempting ($21\,025\to 7\,326$ on a
depth-$8$ tower), which is the whole reason to record it here.

\subsection{Collapsing the pursuit record}

Of the fields a pursuit carries, \code{is\_until} and \code{x\_mu} are functions
of the modality, and \code{rolled} is recoverable from the deadline (which is
$b$ before the first witness and $b-a$ after; when $a=0$ the two coincide and so
do the guards). All three are therefore \emph{functionally determined}, and
dropping them from the key merges nothing --- a field determined by $\mu$ takes
the same value at every node carrying $\mu$. Only \code{part} is genuinely
recoverable from data that varies, and only when the zone lies wholly in one
part of the window; canonicalising it there is worth a few per cent on timed
releases and nothing elsewhere.

\subsection{Goal subsumption}

$\mathrm{Q}(\varphi\,\mathbf{R}_I\,\psi)$ with $0\in I$ asserts $\psi$ at this
configuration, and a disjunction is implied by any disjunct already present, so
both consequences can be dropped from $\Gam$. Worth $8\%$ on towers and $36\%$
on shapes that actually carry the redundancy; roughly neutral on the suite.

One trap: the first version dropped \emph{literals} too, and
$\mathrm{EG}(\mathrm{AF}\,p\wedge\mathrm{AG}\,\neg p)$ came back satisfiable.
Closure is decided on $\Gam_{\mathsf{prop}}$, so a literal removed here is a
literal the contradiction test cannot see, and the release only restores it at
the next expansion, by which time the complementary literal may have been
consumed.

\subsection{Treating $\mathrm{AG}\,\psi$ as context}

$\mathrm{AG}\,\psi\equiv\psi\wedge\mathrm{AX}\,\mathrm{AG}\,\psi$, and the
calculus already carries ``holds at every successor''. Encoding it that way
removes a live pursuit per nesting level. It is a large win where such
modalities sit \emph{side by side} ($106\,679\to 14\,809$ nodes on six parallel
$\mathrm{AG}(\mathrm{EF}\cdots)$) and a growing loss where one sits
\emph{inside} another ($-50\%$ at tower depth $9$), because an $\mathrm{AG}$
under an until is re-demanded through the context at every onset that until
relaunches. Restricting it to modalities under no enclosing until keeps the wins
and caps the loss at $8\%$. Left behind a flag, default off; the regression is
real and unbounded in depth.

\subsection{Conflict-driven anything}

See \cref{sec:problem}: zero rejected and zero closed nodes on the hardest
instance. There are no conflicts to learn from.
