\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

Two searches are selectable at the command line: \code{v1} is the baseline with
the optional optimisations off, \code{v2} adds both decomposition forms and goal
subsumption. Both carry the soundness fix of \S\ref{sec:engineering}, which is
not an optimisation. All measurements are single-threaded on one machine; the
medians of the suite families sit near $8$--$10$\,ms and are dominated by process
start-up, so only $p95$, maxima and the large instances carry signal.

everywhere.

\paragraph{Verdicts.} A suite of $5000$ formulas over ten structural families
carries expected verdicts wherever a window-containment argument settles them.
\code{v1} and \code{v2} agree on all $5000$, and neither contradicts an
expectation. A $700$-case sweep over
$\AG(\EF_{[a,b]}p)\wedge\AG_{[c,d]}\neg p$, whose verdict is decided by an
inequality on $(a,b,c,d)$, is exact for both. The regression suite passes
$861/861$ checks under each. A witness automaton is extracted and structurally
validated for all $4141$ satisfiable instances.

\paragraph{The cost where it does not fire.} A second suite, of ten families of
$220$ instances, exists to break \code{v2} rather than to flatter it: families
one symbol away from decomposing, chains whose transitive closure is a single
component, independence buried under a nest, components that decompose again,
deep towers, constants across six orders of magnitude, punctual intervals, data
variables, and conjunction widths up to $80$. Across all $2200$ instances there
is no wrong verdict, no crash, no \code{v2} timeout and no instance where
\code{v2} is materially slower. \code{v1} exhausts its budget on $200$ of them
and \code{v2} answers every one, with median speed-ups of $63\times$ on the
widest conjunctions and $11\times$ on recursively decomposing ones.

What it did find is a tax. On the families built so that decomposition never
fires, \code{v2} was $9$--$11\%$ slower, consistently enough at $0.1$--$0.3$\,s
runtimes not to be measurement noise. Hoisting three per-call allocations out of
the partition, indexing the symbol memo by dense formula identifier, and the two
changes noted in \S\ref{sec:algorithm} bring it to $2$--$6\%$: the ratio on a
depth-$9$ tower moves from $0.91$ to $0.98$, and on the shared-symbol family from
$0.89$ to $0.94$.

\paragraph{Threats to validity.} The family that decomposes is one we wrote, and
it has exactly the shape the technique targets; the defensible claim is about
the mechanism and not about a speed-up on a workload. Conjunctions of
independent properties are common in practice --- a safety property and a
liveness property over different signals --- but this suite is not evidence for
how common. The root-level form is preprocessing, and splitting a top-level
conjunction is close to running the tool once per conjunct; we report it
separately for that reason. Finally, witness extraction is disabled under
decomposition, a witness for a decomposed node being the product of its
components' automata, which the extractor does not build.
