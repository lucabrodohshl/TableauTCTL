\multirow{4}{*}{\shortstack{\textbf{A} \\Depth Scaling}}
& Untimed Baseline
& Clock-free reference formula with alternating untimed modalities (e.g\ AG/EF), used as a baseline for structural scaling without timed operators. \\

& Overlap SAT
& Satisfiable conjunction of overlapping timed eventualities of the form
  $\bigwedge_i EF_{[i,i+M]}\,p_i$,
  where overlapping windows force zone interaction but each obligation remains schedulable. \\

& Nested EU
& Increasing-depth nesting of bounded existential until,
  e.g.\ $E(\mathit{true}\ U_{[1,M]}\ E(\mathit{true}\ U_{[1,M]}\ \cdots))$,
  accumulating timed eventuality obligations. \\

& Killed UNSAT
& Unsatisfiable variant in which a global invariant blocks every target atom, e.g.\
  $\left(\bigwedge_i EF_{[i,i+M]} p_i\right) \wedge AG\,\bigwedge_i \neg p_i$. \\

\midrule

\multirow{7}{*}{\shortstack{\textbf{B} \\Max-Constant \\Sensitivity}}
& EU Delay
& Bounded-until benchmark of the form
  $E(\neg p_0\ U_{[0,M]}\ p_0)$,
  where satisfaction requires symbolic delay and only the maximal constant $M$ varies. \\

& Deadline UNSAT
& Deadline conflict between a reachability demand and a timed prohibition, e.g.\
  $EF_{[1,M]} p_0 \wedge AG_{[0,M]} \neg p_0$,
  making the formula unsatisfiable. \\

& Negated Window
& Immediate timed contradiction
  $EF_{[0,M]} p_0 \wedge \neg EF_{[0,M]} p_0$,
  stressing normalisation of negated bounded modalities. \\

& Window Chain
& Sequential chain of bounded windows partitioning the interval $[0,M]$, e.g.\
  $\bigwedge_{j=1}^{k} EF_{[a_j,b_j]} p_j$
  with consecutive segments and fixed structure as only endpoints grow. \\

& Nested Chain
& Fixed-depth nested bounded chain,
  e.g.\ $E(\mathit{true}\ U_{[0,M]}\ E(\mathit{true}\ U_{[0,M]}\ \cdots))$,
  isolating the impact of increasing $M$ on extrapolation. \\

& Forced Delay
& Reachability formula forcing delay close to the upper bound, e.g.\
  $EF_{[M-1,M]} p_0$,
  with extrapolation enabled. \\

& No Extrapolation
& Ablation of the forced-delay case using the same formula
  $EF_{[M-1,M]} p_0$,
  but with extrapolation disabled, exposing uncontrolled zone growth. \\

\midrule

\multirow{2}{*}{\shortstack{\textbf{C} \\Concurrent Timed\\ Obligations}}
& Staggered
& Conjunction of timed eventualities over distinct, non-overlapping windows,
  e.g.\ $\bigwedge_i EF_{[i,i+2]} p_i$,
  isolating clock concurrency without overlap. \\

& Identical Windows
& Conjunction of timed eventualities over the same window,
  e.g.\ $\bigwedge_i EF_{[1,3]} p_i$,
  maximising overlap between active timing constraints. \\

\midrule

\multirow{2}{*}{\shortstack{\textbf{D} \\Until Nesting}}
& Nested Until
& Increasing-depth nesting of existential bounded until at fixed $M$,
  e.g.\ $E(\phi_1\ U_{[0,M]}\ E(\phi_2\ U_{[0,M]}\ \cdots))$. \\

& Conjunctive Until
& Width-oriented variant with multiple independent existential-until conjuncts,
  e.g.\ $\bigwedge_i E(\phi_i\ U_{[0,M]} \psi_i)$,
  contrasting width with nesting depth. \\

\midrule

\multirow{1}{*}{\shortstack{\textbf{E} \\Release Nesting}}
& Nested Release
& Increasing-depth nesting of timed release,
  e.g.\ $E(\phi_1\ R_{[0,M]}\ E(\phi_2\ R_{[0,M]}\ \cdots))$,
  capturing greatest-fixpoint preservation obligations. \\

\midrule

\multirow{1}{*}{\shortstack{\textbf{DE} \\Mixed\\Until--Release}}
& Until + Release
& Conjunction of nested least- and greatest-fixpoint formulas, combining until and release subformulas in a single benchmark. \\

\midrule

\multirow{3}{*}{\shortstack{\textbf{F} \\Zeno Rejection}}
& Safety Vacuity
& Timed safety formula such as $AG_{[1,M]}\ \mathit{false}$, satisfiable only under Zeno behaviour where time never reaches the interval $[1,M]$. \\

& EG Conflict
& Conflict between infinite-path persistence and timed prohibition, e.g.\
  $EG\,p_0 \wedge AG_{[1,M]}\neg p_0$,
  requiring anti-Zeno rejection for correctness. \\

& Stacked Traps
& Conjunction of many Zeno-sensitive AG/EG conflicts, scaling the number of independent anti-Zeno obligations with $M$. \\

\midrule

\multirow{3}{*}{\shortstack{\textbf{G} \\Clock \\Dimensionality}}
& Conjunctive SAT
& Satisfiable conjunction of many timed obligations introducing $k$ simultaneously active clocks, e.g.\
  $\bigwedge_i EF_{[i,i+2]} p_i$. \\

& Nested Clocks
& $k$-deep nested eventualities of the form
  $E(\mathit{true}\ U_{[0,M]}\ E(\mathit{true}\ U_{[0,M]}\ \cdots))$,
  preventing early clock deallocation and keeping all clocks active. \\

& Conjunctive UNSAT
& Unsatisfiable counterpart of conjunctive clock scaling, obtained by adding a global blocker such as
  $AG\,\bigwedge_i \neg p_i$
  to the satisfiable conjunctive instance. \\

\midrule

\multirow{2}{*}{\shortstack{\textbf{H} \\Eventuality \\Chain Length}}
& Chain SAT
& Satisfiable existential-until chain with fixed $M=2$,
  e.g.\ $E(p_1\ U_{[0,2]}\ E(p_2\ U_{[0,2]}\ \cdots E(p_n\ U_{[0,2]} q)\cdots))$,
  where growth comes only from chain length. \\

& Chain UNSAT
& Unsatisfiable version of the same chain, with the terminal target blocked globally, e.g.\
  $AG\,\neg q$
  conjoined with the chain formula. \\

\midrule

\multirow{3}{*}{\shortstack{\textbf{I} \\Fixpoint Alternation}}
& Pure Safety
& Nesting of universal release only, e.g.\
  $A(\phi_1\ R_{[0,1]}\ A(\phi_2\ R_{[0,1]}\ \cdots))$,
  containing only greatest-fixpoint obligations. \\

& Pure Liveness
& Nesting of universal until only, e.g.\
  $A(\phi_1\ U_{[0,1]}\ A(\phi_2\ U_{[0,1]}\ \cdots))$,
  containing only least-fixpoint/eventuality obligations. \\

& Alternating
& Alternating universal until and release,
  e.g.\ $A(\phi_1\ U_{[0,1]}\ A(\phi_2\ R_{[0,1]}\ A(\phi_3\ U_{[0,1]}\ \cdots)))$,
  creating repeated interaction between liveness and safety constraints. \\

\bottomrule
